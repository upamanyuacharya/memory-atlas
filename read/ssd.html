In plain English

Flash storage — vast and cheap, but thousands of times slower than memory. It holds models and data at rest, never the live work. Runs on its own boom-bust cycle, separate from HBM.

What it is

PCIe Gen5 flash — ~14 GB/s but microsecond latency, 3–5 orders slower than DRAM. Holds weights at rest, checkpoints, last-ditch KV overflow.

The business

NAND oligopoly: Samsung, SK Hynix (Solidigm), Micron, Kioxia, plus controllers. AI lifts enterprise SSD demand but it is a separate cycle from HBM.

Bottleneck severity

Moderate
