In plain English

The little modules that convert electrical signals into light at every switch port. China's InnoLight and Eoptolink build most of them — but the critical brain inside each one is a US-made chip (Marvell). That dependency is the real point of leverage in the whole optics story.

What it is

The pluggable modules that light up every switch port. InnoLight + Eoptolink dominate volume, Coherent is vertically integrated, Fabrinet assembles for everyone — and every module needs a Marvell or Broadcom DSP inside.

The business

The photon moves through Chinese hardware but the brains are US-designed — a Marvell/Broadcom DSP sits in every 800G+ module. That makes a US DSP export control more disruptive than any tariff. Fabrinet is the cleanest pluggable-demand barometer.

Where the chokepoint comes from

Assembly is abundant (China builds ~60% of modules) — but every single module needs a US-designed DSP chip and a scarce laser inside. One export-control decision or one laser shortage stalls the whole segment.
