In plain English

The handful of numbers the GPU is touching this exact instant — like the figures lit up on a calculator's screen. Far too tiny to invest in on its own; the value sits with whoever designs the chip (NVIDIA, AMD) and whoever prints it (TSMC).

What it is

The fastest memory that exists. Each GPU Streaming Multiprocessor holds ~256 KB of register file — the operands of instructions in flight. ~32 MB aggregate on an H100.

The business

Not separately investable — it ships inside the GPU. The economics flow to whoever designs the GPU (NVIDIA, AMD) and the foundry that prints it (TSMC).
